🧠 ADAPTIVE AI ENGINE & SCENARIO ROLE SWAPPING

• Behavioral Tracking: The AI measures enemy Rush Aggression, Turtling, and Gem Hoarding. It adapts between COUNTER-RUSH, OUT-RESOURCE, BED-RUSH, and BALANCED-EXPANSION strategies.
• Dynamic Role Swapping: Units swap roles mid-round (CLUTCH_DEFEND when Bed is threatened, RETREAT_BANK when holding gems or low HP, BOSS_HUNTER when Guardians spawn, RUSH when enemy Bed is exposed).

💎 DIAMOND GUARDIANS (EVERY 50 DUAL / 100 QUAD TURNS)

Every 50 turns in Dual or 100 turns in Quad, neutral Diamond Guardians spawn on Diamond Generators (15 HP, 5 ATK, 3 DEF). Defeating a Guardian awards 10 Diamonds directly to the killer!

🎲 ACCELERATED D20 COMBAT & PLAYBACK CONTROL

Capturing an enemy piece triggers a D20 Combat encounter! Final Damage = (D20 Roll + Attacker ATK) - Defender DEF. Change round speed dynamically from 0.5x to INSTANT via the top bar overlay!
